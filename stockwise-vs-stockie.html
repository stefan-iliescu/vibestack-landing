Quick summary

Stockie is a well-established Shopify inventory app with purchase order management, starting at $4.99/month. Stockwise is a newer app focused on demand forecasting and reorder recommendations, with a unique ability to blend Shopify sales data with offline sales from physical locations or other channels.

If you need purchase orders today, Stockie has them. If you need accurate forecasting that accounts for your full sales picture — including sales outside Shopify — Stockwise is built for that.

Pricing

Stockie starts at $4.99/month for basic features and goes up to $59.99/month for purchase orders and advanced features. Stockwise is a single plan at $29.99/month with all features included.

If you only need basic reorder alerts and are price-sensitive, Stockie's lower entry price is attractive. If you need demand forecasting with offline sales blending, Stockwise offers more at a comparable mid-tier price.

Who should choose Stockwise

  • Merchants who sell at physical locations, markets, or wholesale outside Shopify POS
  • Stores that want to see how this week's sales compare to last week, last month, and last year
  • Merchants who want accurate reorder forecasts based on their full sales picture
  • Stores that need a simple, clean dashboard without a steep learning curve

Who should choose Stockie

  • Merchants who need purchase order creation and management
  • Stores looking for the lowest possible entry price
  • Merchants who need a Built for Shopify certified app
