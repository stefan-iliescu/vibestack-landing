What Stocky did and what you need to replace it

Stocky helped Shopify merchants track inventory levels, create purchase orders, and get basic reorder recommendations. When Shopify shut it down, merchants were left looking for alternatives that could handle at least these core functions.

The good news: the replacements are generally more capable. The tradeoff is that most of them cost money, and they vary significantly in complexity and focus.

Stockie

Best budget option

$4.99 to $59.99/month

Stockie is a well-established Shopify inventory app with purchase order management, reorder points, and basic forecasting. It has a Built for Shopify badge and starts at $4.99/month for basic features, with purchase orders on the $59.99/month plan.

Best for: Merchants on a very tight budget who only need basic alerts. Built for Shopify badge gives it an SEO and trust advantage.

What it doesn't do: Offline sales blending, advanced trend analysis, PDF export, automatic inventory updates on receipt.

Assisty

Best for analytics-heavy teams

Free to $239/month

Assisty offers 300+ reports across inventory, sales, and customers. It has a free plan and broad feature coverage, making it a popular Stocky replacement for merchants who want more than just reorder recommendations.

Best for: Merchants who need broad analytics beyond inventory. Free plan makes it easy to try.

What it doesn't do: Offline sales blending, PDF export, automatic inventory updates on receipt.

Prediko

Best for growing D2C brands

$49 to enterprise/month

Prediko is a full inventory management platform with AI forecasting, purchase orders, raw materials tracking, and 70+ integrations. It targets D2C brands doing significant revenue that need a dedicated ops system.

Best for: Established brands with a dedicated ops team and complex inventory needs.

What it doesn't do: Offline sales blending. Expect a proper onboarding process rather than a simple install.

How to choose

Ask yourself two questions: Do you need purchase orders? And do you sell outside Shopify?

  • If you need purchase orders, forecasting, and offline sales blending in one plan, Stockwise
  • If you need basic alerts only and are very price-sensitive, Stockie
  • If you need broad analytics beyond inventory, Assisty
  • If you're a larger brand with raw materials and ERP integration needs, Prediko
